The way people search is changing. They used to type into Google and scan a list of links; today many ask **ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers** directly: "who's the best dentist near me?" or "recommend a web designer for my business." The AI answers with a handful of names. The key question for your business: **will it recommend you?**

## The short answer

For AI to recommend your business, your information needs to be **clear, consistent and trustworthy** across the web. AI models draw on your website, your Business Profile, your reviews and what others say about you. If your online presence is organized, complete and well structured, your odds of appearing in their answers go up. This is starting to be called **GEO** (generative engine optimization), and it's the natural evolution of SEO.

## Why this matters for your business now

When AI answers, it doesn't show ten options: it shows two or three. Competition to be "the recommendation" is tougher than ever. The business in that answer gets the customer; the rest don't exist for that person. Getting ahead of this now is a big advantage, because most businesses aren't working on it yet.

## How to optimize your website for AI search

### 1. Answer questions directly

AI extracts clear answers. Structure your content around real customer questions and answer each one immediately, in a sentence or two, before going into detail (just like this section).

### 2. Use a clear, organized structure

Descriptive headings, short sections, lists and plain language. Both Google and AI models understand, and cite, well-organized content better than a wall of text.

### 3. Be consistent across your whole presence

Your name, what you do, your location and your details should be **identical** on your website, Google Business Profile, social profiles and directories. AI trusts information that repeats consistently across sources; contradictions count against you.

### 4. Build trust and reputation

Reviews, mentions, testimonials and quality original content. Models weigh reputation: the more consistent positive signals exist about your business, the more likely you are to be recommended.

### 5. Publish genuinely useful content

A blog that answers your customers' real questions gives AI material to understand and cite you. Every well-written article is one more chance to appear in a generated answer.

### 6. Get structured data and technical SEO right

Schema markup, good speed, a mobile-friendly site and indexable pages are still the foundation. AI tools often lean on the same search engines, so solid technical SEO helps in both worlds.

## Traditional SEO vs. AI SEO: you don't choose, you add

This isn't about abandoning classic SEO; it's about extending it. Good traditional SEO (showing up on Google, being fast, having authority) is also the foundation AI uses to find you. The difference is that now, beyond ranking links, you want to **be the source of the answer.** If your business is local, start with the essentials: [showing up on Google Maps](/en/blog/how-to-show-up-on-google-maps/).

## The starting point: a solid website

None of this works on a slow, confusing or incomplete site. AI won't recommend a business whose website doesn't make clear what it does or doesn't build trust. Before chasing the latest trend, make sure your foundation is solid; if you're not sure, see [why your website isn't getting leads](/en/blog/why-your-website-isnt-getting-leads/).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?**
Optimizing your online presence so AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google's AI answers) mention and recommend your business. It's SEO evolving for AI-driven search.

**Is traditional SEO dead?**
The opposite: it's still the foundation. AI leans heavily on search engines, so solid SEO helps you in both Google and generated answers.

**How do I know if AI is recommending my business?**
Ask ChatGPT or Gemini directly about your type of service in your area and see if you appear. Repeat it periodically to track progress.

**How long does it take?**
Like SEO, it isn't instant. Building presence, reviews and consistent content takes weeks or months, but starting early is a big advantage because few businesses are doing it.
